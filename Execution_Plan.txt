T07 — M3

Write AI algorithm sheet.

Easy AI
- ROCK: 33.33%
- PAPER: 33.33%
- SCISSORS: 33.33%
- no history
- no pattern tracking
- no adaptation

Normal AI
- record current-match player moves
- select counter to most frequent player move
- counter probability: 50%
- remaining two moves: 25% each

Hard AI
- record current-match player moves
- weight recent three moves higher
- predict likely next player move
- favor counter to predicted move
- counter probability: 60%
- remaining two moves: 20% each

Add AI scope rule:
- AI behavior is identical across Best-of-3, Best-of-5, Best-of-7, Best-of-9, Custom, Unlimited

T21 — M3

Implement Hard AI:
- record all current-match player moves
- assign weight 4 to latest move
- assign weight 3 to second latest move
- assign weight 2 to third latest move
- assign weight 1 to every older move
- predicted move equals highest weighted score
- tied prediction uses latest move among tied
- counter probability 60%
- remaining two moves 20% each
- no history uses equal random
